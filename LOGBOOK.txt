2026-10-20 23:28 HKT — situation review, critic, Jev, then a plateau
------------------------------------------------------------------------

What was asked
  Find situations where Mandate breaks for a customer or for the policy,
  judge them, fix, and stop when another pass does not move the score.

Jev
  POST https://api.typesafe.ai/v1/systemone with model jev-latest.
  The host answered 401 authentication_error both times it was called.
  TYPESAFE_API_KEY is not set here. The xAI key is not accepted by that host.
  No Jev probability, label, or score was invented.
  The pass/fail oracle is the executable suite in src/lib/gate.security.test.ts
  and src/lib/policy.test.ts. Two critic reviews read the gate separately.

Situations that were wrong before this pass
  1. The same idempotency key paid again after the receipt scrolled off the 40-row ledger.
  2. The same key with a different body returned the first receipt and hid the mismatch.
  3. needs_approval did not hold the daily limit or the available balance, so several approvals could oversubscribe the day.
  4. After a human approved, a retry of the key still said needs_approval.
  5. Pending could grow without a cap.
  6. Vendor.near and vendor.near did not match. A non-ASCII lookalike could be saved.
  7. A short 0x string could be saved as a recipient. Amounts above 1_000_000 and a rounded $0.00 dust amount could be allowed.
  8. A failed or unfinished OutLayer dry-run still debited the demo balance.
  9. HTTP 200 with would_succeed false (OutLayer's bridge refusal) was treated as success and spent.
  10. HTTP 429 and 5xx were stored as permanent refusals, so a blip could not be retried.
  11. A UTC day change left yesterday's approval able to spend today's budget.
  12. POST /api/v1/policy, /fund, and /approvals were open to anyone who could reach the server, including an agent that was only supposed to call /pay.
  13. Those human routes returned 200 {} because the handlers did not await the gate.
  14. After Approve, the ledger still showed the old Needs approval row, including a withdraw path.
  15. The balance-reject sentence quoted the held amount as if it were the wallet balance.
  16. One double-click could send two payments. The buttons did not send an idempotency key.
  17. An unfinished dry-run during Approve pinned the idempotency key to the failure and dropped the hold.
  18. Freezing the wallet left holds in place.
  19. A few hundred policy rejects could fill the idempotency log and block a later real payment.

What changed
  Holds reserve today's limit and the available balance. Pending is capped at 20.
  Idempotency stores the receipt itself, and only for an allow, a hold, or a settlement refusal.
  A different body on the same key is 409 conflict. Prepare demo clears keys and drops open holds.
  A UTC day change or a freeze cancels waiting payments and releases the hold.
  Human HTTP routes are 403 unless MANDATE_HUMAN_TOKEN is set, and 401 if the header does not match.
  The handlers await the gate and return the snapshot.
  The open Needs approval row is removed from the ledger when it is approved, rejected, or cleared.
  Dust under one cent is rejected. EVM recipients must be 20 bytes. .near is case-insensitive.
  OutLayer is accepted only when would_succeed is true. 429 and 5xx stay retryable.
  An unfinished check during Approve puts the hold back.
  The pay buttons ignore a second click until the first request finishes.

Score
  Round 1 critic, after the first fix set: 6 open failures (ledger ghost, human HTTP 200 {}, dust allow, misleading balance sentence, double-click, approval dry-run pinning the key).
  Those 6 were fixed and covered by tests.
  Round 2 critic: no remaining high-severity bypass of the local policy when OUTLAYER_API_KEY is unset. One high hole remained: a 200 body with would_succeed false still spent. One medium: 429/5xx were sticky. One low: 200 stored keys brick new keys.
  Those three were fixed. Policy rejects are no longer stored, so a reject flood does not fill the log. The cap of 200 still applies to stored allows, holds, and settlement refusals, and it fails closed. That is the plateau. A third critic pass was not started.
